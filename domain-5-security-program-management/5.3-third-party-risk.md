# 5.3 - Third-Party Risk Management

**Official CompTIA SY0-701 objective:** 5.3 - Explain the processes associated with third-party risk assessment and management.
**Recommended resource:** Professor Messer SY0-701 - "Third-Party Risk Management", "Agreements" (Domain 5, Section 5.3)

---

## Why Third-Party Risk is Critical

Your security is only as strong as your weakest vendor. Third parties with access to your systems or data extend your attack surface.

**Examples of vendor-based compromises:**
- SolarWinds (2020) - malicious update distributed to 18,000+ organizations
- Kaseya VSA (2021) - ransomware distributed through an MSP to 1,500+ businesses

---

## Vendor Assessment

| Method | Description |
|---|---|
| **Penetration testing** | Directly test the vendor's security posture |
| **Right-to-audit clause** | Contractual clause giving the right to audit the vendor's security |
| **Evidence of internal audits** | SOC 2 Type II report, ISO 27001 certificate |
| **Independent assessments** | Third-party security ratings (BitSight, SecurityScorecard) |
| **Supply chain analysis** | Evaluate the vendor's own vendors |
| **Security questionnaire** | Standardized questionnaire (CAIQ, SIG) sent to the vendor |

---

## Vendor Selection

| Step | Description |
|---|---|
| **Due diligence** | Thoroughly investigate before signing - questionnaires, certifications, references |
| **Conflict of interest** | The auditor must not also be the vendor's consultant |
| **Vendor diversity** | Avoid excessive dependence on a single vendor (single vendor lock-in) |

---

## Agreement Types

| Agreement | Purpose | Exam importance |
|---|---|---|
| **SLA** (Service Level Agreement) | Defines performance standards (uptime, response time) | Highly tested |
| **MOA** (Memorandum of Agreement) | Formal agreement between parties | |
| **MOU** (Memorandum of Understanding) | Defines mutual intention to work together toward common goals | |
| **MSA** (Master Service Agreement) | Framework contract governs the relationship and legal terms for all future, repeated transactions| |
| **SOW** (Statement of Work) | Details the scope of work, location, deliverables, project timeline, milestones | |
| **NDA** (Non-Disclosure Agreement) | Protects confidential information shared with the vendor | |
| **BPA** (Business Partners Agreement) | Covers the ongoing business relationship | |

> **SLA is the most tested.** Know that violations have financial consequences. Guaranteed uptime: 99.9% = ~8.7 hours downtime/year, 99.99% = ~52 minutes/year.

---

## Ongoing Vendor Management

| Activity | Description |
|---|---|
| **Vendor monitoring** | Continuous evaluation, not just at contract signing |
| **Questionnaires** | Annual security questionnaire updates |
| **Rules of engagement** | Defines purpose and scope,  Makes everyone aware of the test parameters |
| **Offboarding** | Formal process for ending a vendor relationship - revoke access, retrieve/erase data |

---

## Practice Questions

**Q: An organization requires all its cloud vendors to provide a SOC 2 Type II report annually. What type of vendor assessment is this?**
Answer: **Evidence of internal audits** - SOC 2 Type II attests that controls operated effectively over a given period (typically 6-12 months).

**Q: A contract specifies that the vendor must maintain 99.99% monthly availability. What type of agreement contains this clause?**
Answer: **SLA (Service Level Agreement)**.

**Q: Why is it important to assess a vendor's own vendors (supply chain analysis)?**
Answer: A trusted vendor may itself have been compromised through its own vendors (supply chain attack). The chain of trust extends to all sub-contractors.
